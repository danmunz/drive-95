## Design Context

### Users
One adult and an 11-year-old passenger driving north from Jacksonville, Florida, to Alexandria, Virginia. They need to make a quick, low-stress decision about a reliable stop while in transit, usually on a phone.

### Brand Personality
Calm, practical, and gently friendly. The experience should create confidence rather than novelty or urgency.

### Aesthetic Direction
Plain and utilitarian mobile road book with a small amount of warmth in its colors and type. It should favor clear route order, large controls, and immediate action over decorative UI, editorial framing, or unusual interaction patterns.

### Design Principles
- Put the next useful decision before secondary information.
- Make every stop actionable in one or two taps.
- Use recognizable, busy, well-serviced destinations and state the practical tradeoff.
- Keep the visual system quiet, high-contrast, and readable one-handed.
- Respect passenger safety: use the site before pulling off the road or while parked.
